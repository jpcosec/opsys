# Operationalize inbox and drawer promotion

ID: task-operationalize-inbox-drawer-promotion
Status: deferred
Priority: medium

## Goal

Make promotion from inbox and drawer into active tasks explicit and repeatable.

## Scope

- inbox note to drawer question/issue/task candidate
- drawer feature/issue/question to active task
- graph finding to review item
- rejected finding to recorded decision

## Done When

- Promotion criteria are enforced by routine or CLI behavior instead of manual convention alone.
