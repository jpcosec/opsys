# Prove atom materialization on a small end-to-end example slice

ID: feature-007
Status: promoted

## Goal

Demonstrate the full workflow from atom to derived docs, feature, task, and pill on a minimal example so the methodology becomes concrete.

## Why

The design will remain abstract until one concept is actually carried through the whole path from durable conceptual source into planning and execution artifacts.

## Scope

In scope: choosing one small conceptual slice, materializing it into at least one durable doc plus one drawer feature, one desk task, and one pill, and checking traceability. Out of scope: migrating the whole repo at once.

## Proposed Shape

Pick one atom-sized concept and derive a stable doc or spec from it, a deferred feature for planning, an executable task for implementation, and a pill for temporary execution context. Use the result to validate the edge model and expose missing CLI or modeling surfaces.

## Adoption Path

Promoted into active execution and now represented by the example atom, derived doc, pill, materializers, and tests.

## Validation

- One atom is materialized into multiple artifact phases.
- Traceability is preserved from source atom to every derivative.
- At least one desk task and one pill are produced from the same atom lineage.
- The proof run reveals concrete tooling gaps or confirms the workflow.
