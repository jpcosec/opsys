# Self reflection is a feedback loop

ID: atom-self-reflection-is-a-feedback-loop
5WH1+: what

## Answer

Self reflection means the system regularly compares its current artifacts, operations, failures, and user questions against its atoms and issues, then writes missing knowledge as atoms and unresolved uncertainty as routed issues.
