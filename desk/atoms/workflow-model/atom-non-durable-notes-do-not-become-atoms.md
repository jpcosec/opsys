# Non durable notes do not become atoms

ID: atom-non-durable-notes-do-not-become-atoms
5WH1+: what

## Answer

Transient instructions, task-local observations, weak inferences, duplicated prose, and relation indexes should not become atoms because they do not preserve one stable reusable answer.
