# Workflow surfaces have operational lifetimes

ID: atom-workflow-surfaces-have-operational-lifetimes
5WH1+: what

## Answer

A workflow surface is any repository artifact that routes, preserves, generates, or explains work; each surface must be understood by its lifetime as active, deferred, durable, generated, or transient.
