# Drawers feed tasks through promotion

ID: atom-drawers-feed-tasks-through-promotion
5WH1+: how

## Answer

Future, uncertain, or loosely shaped work enters `desk/drawer/` as features, issues, questions, or other lax artifacts, then becomes active execution only when explicitly promoted into routed tasks.
