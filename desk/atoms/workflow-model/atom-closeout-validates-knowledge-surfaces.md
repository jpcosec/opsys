# Closeout validates knowledge surfaces

ID: atom-closeout-validates-knowledge-surfaces

## Answer

Before closeout, a task should check whether changed docs, specs, diagrams, code, tests, inbox items, drawer items, and atoms are consistent, routed, deleted, or intentionally left as follow-up work.
