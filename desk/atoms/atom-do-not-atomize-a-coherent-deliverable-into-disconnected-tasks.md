# Do not atomize a coherent deliverable into disconnected tasks

## Answer

Do not split one coherent deliverable into several tasks so each looks small. The pieces then need synthetic dependencies and synthetic evidence to look complete, and the work that mattered (the integration) has no owner. One task per deliverable; use phases for layers of separate deliverables.
