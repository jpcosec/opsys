# Protoatoms are free atoms captured before typing

## Answer

A protoatom (ProtoAtomDoc) is a free knowledge unit: a title, free markdown content and namespaced tags. Unlike an atom it answers no fixed 5WH1+ question. It is the lowest rung of typing: nodes captured in a brainstorm or mindmap are protoatoms until someone types them into a concrete sldb model.
