# Knowledge management

## Content

How project knowledge is captured and projected in deskops. Atoms distill project reality into small durable units; specs formalize selected atoms as contracts; diagrams project their relations visually; docs arrange them into narratives for humans; and implementation feedback decides, when something drifts, whether the atom or its materialization is wrong.
