# Review attestations state only what was actually read

## Answer

Do not attest a review that did not happen. A disposition, ledger or handoff says who read what, and nothing more: a drafting agent never writes that the coordinator reviewed. Do not accept a review from its ambiguity list or from the word none; compare reformulation and dry-run with the full contract. Do not classify something as future work unless its named producer assumes it: a consumer does not create its producer's obligation. Correct earlier records by dated supersession, never by rewriting or backfilling them.
