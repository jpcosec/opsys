# Pattern: operator manual follows stable runnable slices

## What

Write the end-to-end operator manual only after the underlying runnable workflow slices are stable enough to document coherently.

## Why

A large manual written too early calcifies stale workflow assumptions and becomes a second drifting source of truth.

## When

Apply this pill whenever a task proposes consolidating methodology across first use, task flow, atoms, graph work, materialization, drift checks, and closeout.

## Where

Applies to the future operator manual, cross-linking durable guides, and sequencing work between implementation slices and broad documentation.

## How

Document the stable runnable path, link out to narrower guides where needed, and defer unresolved workflow areas to explicit backlog items rather than smoothing over them in prose.

## How Not

Do not write one grand manual to paper over unstable commands or undefined workflow contracts. Do not let documentation pretend the workflow is settled before the runtime is.
